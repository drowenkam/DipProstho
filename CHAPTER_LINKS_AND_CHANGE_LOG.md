### What changed in the website

- The reader now opens as a **14-chapter study directory**, with expandable subsection lists and immediate buttons for reading or practising linked SBAs.
- Each topic has individual direct question links and a **practice this subsection** link; an additional whole-chapter link reaches **every question assigned to that chapter block** (all 1,500 across 14 blocks). All use original question IDs and answers, and return to the matching section page.
- Volume II explanations that match a chapter topic link to that particular notebook page, not merely the start of a 14-unit block.
- The original 105 images remain byte-for-byte identical in the optimized page bundle; 15 appendix pages are appended.
- iPhone/iPad page-reader controls, notes bookmarks, questions, question scoring and saved SBA progress keys are preserved.
- Pages 1-105 remain at their prior numbers, so saved page bookmarks do not shift.

### Editorial limitations

- No question answer key or question text was modified by this structural revision.
- The 1,500-item bank still contains AI-assisted draft questions whose clinical accuracy and examiner relevance require independent specialist review.
- Some topic links are probabilistic and may be broadly related rather than being strict exam syllabus mappings. Review the list before treating it as a validated blueprint.
- The PDF is a page-faithful original plus appendix, not a complete re-typesetting of each original chapter.
